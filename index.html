Workshop Curriculum

What You'll Learn

01

Understand AI

Shift 1

What is AI, really? Cut through the hype. Learn how large language models work, what they can (and can't) do, and why this matters for your career.

02

Learn the Tools

Shift 2

Hands-on with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and more. Master prompt engineering, learn the workflows used by top professionals, and build your AI toolkit.

03

Build Something Real

Shift 3

Put it all together. Build a real AI-powered project from scratch during the workshop. Walk out with something you can show off.

Who Is This For?

College students curious about AI & its real-world applications
Beginners who want to start using AI tools the right way
Anyone who wants to build something real with AI — not just watch tutorials
No coding experience needed — we'll guide you from zero to building
